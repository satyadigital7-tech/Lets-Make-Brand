# Lets Make Brand (LMB) - Partner & Team Member User Guide

## 1. Introduction for Partners

Whether you join as an **Independent Partner** or as a **Vendor Squad Team Member**, LMB provides a unified partner dashboard designed to track campaigns, generate referral links, submit customer leads, and receive direct commission deposits.

---

## 2. Partner Journey & Onboarding

1. **Sign Up:** Register at `/register/partner` or via an invitation link provided by your Vendor Agency.
2. **Account Status:** Initial registrations are placed in `pending_approval`.
3. **Approval & LMB ID:** Once approved, your unique Partner ID (`PTR-XXXXXX`) is issued.
4. **KYC & Banking:** Navigate to `/dashboard/partner/kyc` and submit:
   - Full Legal Name (must match bank account name)
   - PAN details
   - Bank Account Number & IFSC Code, or UPI ID
   - Supporting ID Document

---

## 3. Promoting Campaigns & Generating Links (`/dashboard/partner/links`)

- **Campaign Catalog:** Browse active campaigns at `/dashboard/partner/campaigns` to view payout rates, requirements, and target customer profiles.
- **Custom Link Builder:**
  1. Go to **Promotional Links** (`/dashboard/partner/links`).
  2. Select your target campaign.
  3. (Optional) Enter a **Sub-ID** (e.g. `youtube_short_1`, `insta_bio`) to track traffic sources separately.
  4. Copy your unique link or click **Download High-Res QR** for print and offline promotions.

---

## 4. Submitting & Tracking Leads (`/dashboard/partner/leads`)

- **Submitting Customer Leads:** When a customer is ready to convert, click **Submit New Lead**, choose the campaign, and enter customer details (Full Name, Mobile, Email, and dynamic campaign fields).
- **Status Progression:**
  - `submitted`: Received by compliance moderation queue.
  - `under_review`: Compliance team is validating the customer details.
  - `approved`: Lead successfully qualified! Commissions are immediately credited to your wallet.
  - `resubmission_required`: Corrections needed (e.g., re-upload document).
  - `rejected`: Ineligible or fraudulent lead (with explanation notes).

---

## 5. Wallet & Direct Withdrawals (`/dashboard/partner/wallet`)

- **Earnings:** Commissions accrue in real-time upon lead approval.
- **Requesting Payout:**
  1. Once available balance reaches ₹100, click **Request Withdrawal**.
  2. Choose your saved bank account or enter your UPI ID.
  3. Enter withdrawal amount and confirm.
  4. Track the request status under **Transaction History**.

---

## 6. Official ID & Offer Letter (`/dashboard/partner/documents`)

- **LMB Partner ID Card:** Generate and download your official vector PDF ID card with your unique LMB code and QR code.
- **Appointment Letter:** Download your official formal Partner Engagement Agreement with company letterhead.
