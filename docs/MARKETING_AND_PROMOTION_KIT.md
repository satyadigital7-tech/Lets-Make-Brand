## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 6.1 For Brand Vendors
**Q: How does LMB ensure lead quality?**  
A: Every campaign supports dynamic required qualification fields. Additionally, our automated anti-fraud engine screens submissions against duplicate phone numbers, email addresses, and IP subnets in real time before human verification.

**Q: Can I set my own commission splits with my team?**  
A: Yes. Vendors configure permitted distributions between the agency override and the partner channel. The system maintains immutable version history for all split changes.

**Q: How are agency overrides paid out?**  
A: Vendors submit withdrawal requests from their Corporate Wallet. Once reviewed, the LMB admin initiates a direct NEFT/RTGS bank transfer and uploads the bank UTR number.

### 6.2 For Promotional Partners
**Q: What is the difference between an Independent Partner and a Squad Member?**  
A: Independent Partners register on their own and receive 100% of the Super Admin configured partner rate. Squad Members join under a Vendor agency using a team invite link and work within that agency's configured split.

**Q: When can I withdraw my earnings?**  
A: Once your KYC is verified and your available balance meets the minimum threshold (₹500 for Partners, ₹1,000 for Vendors), you can request a withdrawal at any time.

**Q: How do I generate my official LMB ID card?**  
A: Once your KYC application is approved by compliance, your high-resolution vector PDF ID card and Appointment Letter are generated automatically in your Documents tab (`/dashboard/partner/documents`).
